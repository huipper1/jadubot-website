**Adhere to message frequency limits**: Instagram’s API limits the number of messages you can send in a short period. Exceeding these limits may block your account from sending DMs. Spread out mass campaigns and avoid sending identical messages to large groups.

**Use approved templates for outside 24‑hour window**: After the first 24 hours of a customer’s last interaction, you may need to use pre‑approved message templates for promotional communications (the rules vary depending on the API; check Meta’s latest documentation).

### Ethical chatbot practices

**Be transparent**: Inform users that they are interacting with a bot. Many customers appreciate knowing they’re chatting with an automated system.

**Provide a human option**: Always give users an option to speak to a human for complex issues. Automation should assist, not hinder, human service.

**Avoid manipulative language**: Do not pressure customers with false scarcity or misleading claims. Build trust through accurate information.

**Respect cultural norms**: Bangladesh has diverse cultural and religious practices. Consider local customs when designing your messages. For example, avoid sending promotional messages during certain religious observances.

### Complying with cross‑border regulations

If you sell to customers outside Bangladesh, be aware of data protection regulations like the EU’s **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Provide opt‑out options and remove data upon request.
